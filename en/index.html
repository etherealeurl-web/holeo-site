Holéo is neither butter nor margarine. It is a plant-based oil with a butter flavour, designed to replace butter in everyday cooking.

Thanks to its natural flavouring, Holéo brings the taste of butter to your dishes without the downsides of animal fats. It keeps for 12 months sealed and 3 months after opening, at ambient temperature.

Salt-free, lactose-free, gluten-free, palm oil-free and preservative-free. Suitable for vegans and people with lactose intolerance.

A natural source of omega-3 (rapeseed), omega-6 (sunflower) and vitamin E, Holéo withstands cooking up to 170°C without burning.

Ingredients: refined sunflower oil (France or EU), refined rapeseed oil (France or EU), natural flavouring (EU).

Allergens: may contain traces of nuts and sesame seeds. Lactose-free, gluten-free.

The fruit of an alliance

Holéo was born from the meeting of two French family businesses: Food Ingredients, a specialist in flavourings and flavoured oils, and the Huilerie de Lapalisse, a plant-based oil producer for over a century.

Together, they developed a simple and delicious alternative to butter, combining expertise in natural flavourings with oil-making know-how.

Holéo is a plant-based oil with a butter flavour, made from only 3 natural ingredients. It is neither butter nor margarine: it is a 100% plant-based alternative, designed to replace butter in cooking without its drawbacks (cholesterol, refrigeration, lactose).

Holéo contains only 3 ingredients: refined sunflower oil, refined rapeseed oil and natural flavouring. That's all. Salt-free, lactose-free, gluten-free, palm oil-free and preservative-free. Suitable for vegans and people with lactose intolerance.

Holéo replaces butter in all uses: frying, roasting, baking, seasoning and even in your Airfryer. You can use up to 40% less fat compared to butter. Holéo withstands cooking up to 170°C without burning or smoking.

Holéo keeps for 12 months sealed and 3 months after opening, at ambient temperature. No refrigeration needed: simply store it in your cupboard, away from light.

Holéo is available in supermarkets and through specialist distributors in France. Click on "Where to find us?" to locate the nearest stockist to you.

Per 100 ml: energy value 3404 kJ (828 kcal), fat 92 g of which 8.4 g saturated fatty acids, 44 g monounsaturated and 40 g polyunsaturated. Thanks to the sunflower-rapeseed blend, Holéo is naturally a source of unsaturated fatty acids (omega-3 and omega-6), which contribute to maintaining normal cholesterol levels.
